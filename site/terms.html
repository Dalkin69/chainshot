What ChainShot proves, and what it does not

  • ChainShot makes evidence tamper-evident: it lets anyone detect that a recorded file, entry or seal was changed. It does not make tampering impossible.
  • It records what was displayed; it does not certify that the content was true, nor the integrity of the system observed.
  • Entry times come from the examiner's computer clock; RFC 3161 seals give an independent time for sealed entries.
  • Whether a capture is admissible as evidence depends on the law, the court or authority and the procedure followed. ChainShot and this website do not provide legal advice.
